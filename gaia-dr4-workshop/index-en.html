Gaia DR4 Workshop

September 22, 2026 · Universidad Diego Portales · Santiago, Chile

The Gaia DR4 Workshop will be held on September 22, 2026, at Universidad Diego Portales in Santiago, Chile. The meeting will combine short presentations on Gaia and the forthcoming Data Release 4 with hands-on sessions focused on tools for exoplanet science.

Practical information

  • Date: Tuesday, September 22, 2026.
  • Start: 09:30 AM.
  • Venue: Nicanor Parra Auditorium, Vergara 324, Santiago, Floor -1.
  • Coffee breaks and lunch: provided for participants.

Before the workshop

The hands-on sessions will focus on how to use the tools rather than on installation. Participants are therefore kindly asked to install the required software before the workshop. The current program and installation instructions are kept in the PDF below so that the latest version can be updated in one place.

For installation issues, bugs, or questions, please contact Andrea Bernardi at andrea.bernardi@mail.udp.cl.
